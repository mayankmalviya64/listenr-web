# Listenr Web Privacy Notice

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Listenr Web stores imported documents and library data locally in your browser. Imported files, document text, and document names are not sent to Listenr or Google Analytics.

## Analytics

Listenr Web uses Google Analytics automatically to understand product usage. It may send:

- Generic app views such as All Files, Recents, Favorites, Recycle Bin, Folder, Tag, and Reader
- Aggregate folder, document, and used-tag counts
- Broad categories inferred locally from document and folder names: Student, Office, Mixed, or Other
- Standard technical information processed by Google Analytics, such as device, browser, approximate location, referral source, and usage timestamps

Raw document names, raw folder names, raw tag names, imported files, and document content are not sent to Google Analytics. Document and folder names are examined only in the browser to infer a broad category, and only that category is sent.

The information is used to understand how people use Listenr, identify broad customer groups such as students or office workers, improve the product, and guide possible future offers or discounts. It is not used to read or profile document contents.
